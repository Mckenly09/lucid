Lucid Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 30 October 2026

Your questions never leave your phone. Lucid's AI runs on your device, not on a server. We cannot see what you ask or what it answers.

This policy explains what information the Lucid mobile app ("Lucid", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. Lucid is published by Harry Mack. If you have a question, email us at lucidstudybuddy@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Your questions, answers, chats, flashcards, quiz and practice scores, notes and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • Camera photos and voice recordings are processed on your phone and deleted right after use.
  • If you create an account, we use Google Firebase to sign you in. It stores your name, email address, profile photo and an account ID.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

2. Information that stays on your phone

Lucid stores the following only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Questions you type, scan or speak, and the AI's answers
  • Chat history, flashcards, review schedules, quiz and practice results
  • Notes and subject packs you add
  • Your app settings and, if you use Lucid without an account, the first name you enter
  • The AI model, voice pack and subject pack files you download

3. Information we collect

Account information

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google or with email and password, Lucid uses Google Firebase Authentication to create and manage your account. Firebase stores:

  • your name, email address and profile photo (from Google, or what you enter);
  • a unique account ID; and
  • for email sign-in, your password, which Firebase stores in a secured (hashed) form. We never see it.

We use this only to sign you in and to keep your data on the phone separate between accounts. Firebase may send you emails needed for your account, such as email verification and password reset.

4. Camera and microphone

Camera (Scan). When you scan a question, the photo is read on your phone using on-device text recognition. Every image file, including the original photo, is deleted as soon as the text is read.

Microphone (Voice). When you ask by voice, speech is turned into text on your phone using an on-device speech model. The recording is not saved or sent anywhere.

Lucid asks for camera and microphone permission only when you first use these features. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

5. When Lucid uses the internet

After setup, Lucid works fully offline. It connects to the internet only to:

Your questions, answers, photos and recordings are never part of these requests. Each service handles data under its own privacy policy: Firebase, Hugging Face, GitHub, Google.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.
  • We do not use your questions to train AI models. We never receive them.

7. Backups

Settings → Export creates a backup file on your phone. It goes only where you choose to save or share it. We do not store backups.

8. How long we keep data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall Lucid. Your Firebase account stays until you delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Delete account and data removes your Firebase account and your chats, cards and scores from the phone.
  • Offline profile: Settings → Remove offline profile removes it and its data.
  • Uninstall: removing Lucid deletes everything it stored on the phone.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Because almost all your data stays on your phone, you control it directly. For anything about your account, email us at lucidstudybuddy@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also ask us to delete your account by email if you can no longer open the app.

10. Security

Data on your phone is kept in Lucid's private app storage, protected by your phone's own security. Sign-in is handled by Firebase; we never store your sign-in token ourselves. All network connections use HTTPS, and downloaded files are checked before use. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked.

11. Students and children

Lucid is made for high school students. It collects only what this policy lists, whatever your age. Lucid is not directed at children under 13. If you are under the age required by your country to agree to this policy yourself, please use Lucid with a parent's or guardian's permission. If a parent believes their child created an account without permission, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top, and the new version will appear in the app with an update. If a change significantly affects how your information is used, we will tell you in the app.
